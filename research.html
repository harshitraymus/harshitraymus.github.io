RESEARCH EXPERIENCE

Through Internships.

May-July 2026 ·
MEMS, Microfluidics & Nanoelectronics(MMNE) Lab,
Center for Research Excellence in Semiconductor Technologies(CREST),
BITS Pilani - Hyderabad Campus, India.
Supervisor: Dr.Sanket Goel

Fabrication and characterization of flexible electronic devices, including Laser-Induced-Graphene(LIG) and Organic Thin Film Transistor(OTFT).

I worked on optimizing Laser-Induced Graphene (LIG) electrodes fabricated using CO2 laser direct writing on polyimide substrates for flexible memristive devices. I made reproducible water-assisted LIG and compared them to samples made in ambient air to observe whether water-assistance could reduce edge distortion and improve uniform graphene formation by rapidly removing ablated material from local laser interaction zone. I optimised the laser scanning speed and power to fabricate stable and non-deteriorating LIG electrode samples. I also fabricated organic thin-film transistors(OTFT) on ITO(indium tin oxide)-coated substrates using PVP(polyvinylpyrrolidone) dielectric and TIPS-pentacene as soluble semiconductor, to determine the feasibility of fabricating memristors by simple deposition techniques like dropcasting and spin coating. Following fabrication, I performed electrical characterizations using probe station and Source Measure Unit (SMU). I had exposure to semiconductor cleanroom fabrications including photolithography, thin-film deposition technologies like thermal evaporation, sputtering, and electron-beam evaporation, and fabricated devices like MOSCAPs, MOSFETs, and MoS2-based RRAM with gold contacts on silicon wafers.

IUCAA( Inter-University Centre for Astronomy and Astrophysics) Pune, India.
Supervisor: Dr.Suresh Doravari

Investigation of light sources for Advanced LIGO optical levers

I worked on a miniature model of LIGO(Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory) optical lever for precision measurements and optical sensitivity challenges. This involved a cleanroom optical setup with He-Ne lasers, data acquisition using NI-LabVIEW, noise characterization in MATLAB, and mitigation of environmental fluctuations through developing controlled helium-filled acrylic enclosures.

PRL(Physical Research Laboratory) Ahmedabad, India.

Magnetic susceptibility of cuprates

I studied the electronic transport near magnetic quantum criticality in optimally doped cuprates for its unconventional superconductivity, by solving the Bloch-Boltzmann transport equation using a phenomenologically reverse-engineered scattering model and investigated the temperature-linear resistivity.
