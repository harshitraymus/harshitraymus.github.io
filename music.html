THE MUSICAL SIDE

Built around
the keys.

I’m a Trinity Grade 8 pianist and have been involved in live music as a keyboardist. I enjoy the details that make an arrangement come alive — harmony, voicing, texture, transitions and the relationship between parts.

My musical interests also extend to sound design for film and experimenting with reharmonisation, especially finding unexpected colours beyond straightforward root-position chords.
